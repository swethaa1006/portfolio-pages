2019 – 2020
Ludo Star 2I got an opportunity to re-design the entire UI and UX of a casual game Ludo Star 2. I got another opportunity to add my research phase into my design that I had previously done to my process. I started by discussing with the team about the theme of the UI. Then based on the theme I did the research for a month where I did affinity map by understanding the users. From this I got in-depth understanding of the user and using this I came up with home screen and style guide. Based on this I redesigned the UI of the entire app and am designing the new features based on the new theme and improvising it every day and doing user testing by A/B testing!
